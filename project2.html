Advertising Platform Redesign

Leading the redesign of AdAdapted’s internal advertising platform, a critical tool used by the Ad Operations team to manage client campaigns. This project's goal was to improve overall workflow efficiency and platform usability.

Overview

Problem

The original platform was inefficient, requiring up to two hours for an Ad Operations specialist to set up a single campaign. This process was a significant bottleneck, preventing the team from managing more clients and optimizing campaign performance effectively.

Solution

Deliver a complete redesign of the platform using rapid iteration and user-centered design. The new solution streamlines the setup workflow for campaign targeting and tracking, as well as managing ad creatives and templates.

Users

AdAdapted’s Ad Operations team and their supporting engineers.

Impact

The redesigned platform directly contributed to key business outcomes:

A 50%-75% reduction in campaign setup time, allowing the Ad Operations team to reallocate their time to optimizing campaign performance.

Increased revenue in the first two quarters post-launch by enabling the team to manage a higher volume of campaigns.

The platform efficiently manages over 1240 campaigns, 1386 advertisers and agencies, and 112 apps, serving as a core business asset for attracting new partnerships and expanding product offerings.

The platform receives frequent praise from users cross-departmentally for its ease of use, low friction, and consistency alongside industry trends.

Research

The research process was multifaceted, designed to identify pain points and user needs.

I conducted heuristic evaluations of the existing platform and competitor tools, performed in-depth interviews with the Ad Operations team, and mapped out comprehensive user flows. This data-informed design approach ensured the new platform was built with a deep understanding of the users' workflow and their vision for the ideal platform.

Design Process

The design process was highly collaborative, with iterative design in Figma, close conversations with frontend and backend teams, and additional user testing post-initial launch.

We started by establishing a rudimentary design system, including a modern color scheme, typography, and component libraries, then worked to integrate our design with the engineering team’s backend schemas, identifying edge cases, and fine-tuning error messaging. Since the properties of an advertising campaign cascade through campaigns, flights, and line items, we conducted A/B testing to determine how to make the users’ navigation through each step as fluid and efficient as possible.

Since the initial launch, the project has evolved into an ongoing effort to expand functionality. I've led the design and integration of over 50 new features, including:

  1. The design and integration of new tools that support more flight types (web, video, push notifications), nuanced campaign settings, and new product offerings.
  2. Improved user experience and WCAG accessibility through key upgrades, including better support for colorblind users and other quality-of-life improvements that speed up users’ workflow.
  3. Enhanced cross-team collaboration by creating a labeling feature that helps the Reporting and Analytics team compile client insights more effectively, as well as a custom ad unit preview to benefit the Customer Service team.
  4. Innovative solutions like a new in-workflow tool for generating add-to-cart links for specific retailers and for generic recipe ads.
