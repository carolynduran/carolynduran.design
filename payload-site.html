Payload Site Design

One of AdAdapted’s new R&D projects required a simple, intuitive way for clients to build and maintain payloads, a highly technical task typically handled by developers.

Overview

Problem

Payload as a Service (PaaS), expands capabilities for AdAdapted’s patented Add-to-List technology. I was tasked with designing a user-friendly site that made a highly technical process—JSON configuration—accessible to external clients.

Solution

Design and implement a new, complete Payloads site that simplifies the creation and management of payloads. Apply user-centered design to demystify the technical process, allowing non-engineers to control Payload technology with ease.

Users

AdAdapted’s internal engineers, external advertising managers, and client-side engineers.

Impact

The new PaaS site created a tangible business impact by expanding AdAdapted’s product offerings and contributing to new revenue streams:

The product enabled AdAdapted to offer large retailer clients a more customizable and flexible way to add products to customers’ lists, opening new opportunities for innovation within the CPG and grocery ad tech landscape.

Adding PaaS capability to existing product offerings helped to boost ad effectiveness and increase cart conversion for clients, landing the company million-dollar deals.

Research

Since this was a completely new product, we conducted extensive user research to understand the core technological requirements and user needs.

Our research included comparative analysis of other ad tech platforms and code builders, as well as semi-structured interviews with potential clients. We also conducted post-release interviews for additional usability feedback.

This data-informed design approach yielded critical insights:

  1. We needed to provide clearer semantic indicators for Tag Settings and improve the placement of key UI elements.
  2. Advanced users understood the process faster, but needed more guidance like a data type dictionary and in-line validation to prevent errors.
  3. There was an opportunity to create two distinct experiences: a streamlined "Simple Creation" mode for new users and an "Advanced Creation" for more experienced ones. Both had the potential to be equally as user-friendly.

Semi-structured interview methods revealed evidence-based improvements beyond our initial scope, including adding information for campaign statuses, rearranging menu options for greater accessibility, and clarifying the JSON output display. More of our research and its results can be found in AdAdapted Payloads: User Testing Results & Insights [PDF].

Design Process

The design process was highly iterative in Figma, starting with wireframes and a mini design system before building to high-fidelity visuals.

We focused on creating an engaging, almost gamified experience with a dark theme and glowing visual elements. To simplify the JSON configuration, I designed a unique interface that visually replicated the code structure, serving as an educational tool for less experienced users.

We also added the following key features to improve usability:

  1. Real-time statistics, version management, and easy-to-copy implementation code to give users more control over every step of the setup process.
  2. A refined "Simple Creation" mode with in-line validation to guide new users and reduce confusion.
  3. A data-type dictionary built directly into the form to help users understand JSON.
  4. A strategic overhaul of the navigation to provide intuitive access to Campaign Actions, management tools, and other important features.
